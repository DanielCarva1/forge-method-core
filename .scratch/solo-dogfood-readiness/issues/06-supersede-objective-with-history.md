# 06 — Supersede an objective without erasing history

**What to build:** Make a later conversational correction explicitly supersede the active objective, preserve the prior accepted record, and invalidate authority prepared against the old objective or project state.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Bind the initial objective from ordinary chat.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A material correction creates a new accepted objective linked to the objective it supersedes.
- [ ] Historical objectives remain immutable and auditable.
- [ ] Packets, evidence offers, and promotions prepared for the superseded objective fail as stale.
- [ ] Non-material clarification can enrich the current objective without inventing a new product direction.
- [ ] Replacement-agent readback identifies the current objective and the reason for supersession.
